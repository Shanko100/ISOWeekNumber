The Rust standard library (Rust 1.99.1), as linked into WeekNumber.exe
for x86_64-pc-windows-msvc

Taken from the toolchain's own record, share/doc/rust/COPYRIGHT-library.html,
limited to what a Windows MSVC program links: core, alloc, std,
compiler_builtins, panic_abort, unwind and std_detect (all in the Rust
tree), and the crates.io crates cfg-if, hashbrown and rustc-demangle. The
other crates that record lists are used only for other targets or by
Rust's own build tools.
