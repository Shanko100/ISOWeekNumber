The Rust standard library (Rust 1.99.1), as linked into WeekNumber for
macOS (aarch64-apple-darwin) and Linux (x86_64-unknown-linux-musl,
aarch64-unknown-linux-musl)

Taken from the toolchain's own record, share/doc/rust/COPYRIGHT-library.html,
and the license files of the crate versions in the toolchain's
library/Cargo.lock, limited to what these programs link: core, alloc, std,
compiler_builtins, panic_abort, unwind and std_detect (all in the Rust
tree), and the crates.io crates cfg-if, hashbrown, rustc-demangle, libc,
addr2line, gimli, object and memchr. miniz_oxide and adler2, which std uses
too, have their own notices (licenses/miniz_oxide-MIT.txt and
licenses/adler2-0BSD.txt).
